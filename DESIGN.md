# SpeedProof Visual System

## Direction

SpeedProof treats text editing as an electrical signal moving through a precise
macOS instrument. The durable motif is a bounded proof rail: a thin line with
two endpoints and one yellow pulse that travels only between them. It represents
copy, processing, and paste-back without turning the product into a generic AI
chat interface.

## Palette

- Porcelain `#F4F5F7`: daylight page canvas.
- Ink `#111318`: primary text and strong surfaces.
- Panel `#171A20`: the native app and technical demonstrations.
- Electric yellow `#FFD84D`: action, progress, and proof completion.
- Proof blue `#6F8FFF`: links, selections, and the second brand voice.
- Mist `#6C7380`: secondary text on light surfaces.
- Frost `#B9C0CC`: secondary text on dark surfaces.

Yellow is reserved for the active path and primary conversion. Blue marks
inspectable or explanatory content. Neither color becomes a decorative glow.

## Typography

Use the macOS system sans stack for interface copy and a compact editorial serif
only for the italic `Proof` wordmark. Headlines are dense and direct with no
tracking tricks. Body copy stays between 60 and 72 characters per line.

## Components

- Buttons are solid, high-contrast commands with 12px corners.
- Product windows use the proportions and title-bar cues of native macOS.
- Information sections are unframed page bands; cards are reserved for the
  product demonstration, installation steps, and repeated FAQ disclosures.
- Keyboard shortcuts use semantic `kbd` elements.

## Motion

The signature motion is one yellow pulse travelling between two fixed rail
endpoints, followed by the corrected text appearing and a paste confirmation.
All content remains visible without animation and reduced-motion users receive
instant state changes.

## Responsive Rules

Desktop layouts preserve generous horizontal whitespace around the product
window. Below 760px the demonstration becomes a vertical before/after flow,
navigation collapses to the download action, and all controls remain at least
44px tall. Text never relies on horizontal scrolling.
